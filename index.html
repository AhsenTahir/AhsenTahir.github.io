Ongoing work

SASD: Source-Aware Self-Distillation A training-time defense against prompt injection In progress · targeting NeurIPS / ICLR

Detectors and delimiters added at inference fall to an attacker who rewords the payload. SASD pushes provenance into the weights: each teacher sees the task under a different source condition, and the student targets the one that never saw the injected span. Tested against adaptive attacks, not just fixed templates. More.

When Agents Architect: Design-Level Security Anti-Patterns in LLM-Generated Microservice Systems Ahsen Tahir, Francis Palma, Shazil Khan In progress · University of New Brunswick

Do coding agents introduce security flaws where the architecture is decided, rather than in individual lines? The contribution is the unit of analysis — design patterns, not lines — and an agentic detector where static analysis misses the class entirely. More.
