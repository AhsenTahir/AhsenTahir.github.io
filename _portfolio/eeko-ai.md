An agriculture assistant aimed at smallholder farmers, combining Llama 3.2 Vision for pest and disease detection with YOLOv5 for real-time weed identification, and a contextual LLM grounded on the NASA LARC satellite API.

The design constraint that shaped the project was accessibility rather than accuracy: the target users are not comfortable reading English technical output, so an English-to-Urdu speech model sits between the models and the user. It reached 439+ visitors within hours of launch.

**Stack:** Llama 3.2 Vision, YOLOv5, NASA LARC API, Next.js, Hugging Face
